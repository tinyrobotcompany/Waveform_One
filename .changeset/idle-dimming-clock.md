Add optional idle dimming after 1, 2, 5, 10 or a custom 1–240 minutes, alongside
the existing night schedule. When automatically dimmed, the main screen shows
only a clock; touch or music restores the player. Customize and preview the
clock's size, white/green colour and 12/24-hour format. Preferences survive OTA
and restart; idle dimming defaults to off and the existing night schedule is
preserved. Configure these options on the touchscreen's display settings pages.
